Echo Slide Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Русская версия

YOUR NAME / STUDIO ("we") makes Echo Slide, a free puzzle game supported by optional ads.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information ourselves. Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game. The game has no accounts and needs no sign-in.

Advertising

The game can show optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
